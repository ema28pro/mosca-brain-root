3. Neurotransmitter Sign Rules

Acetylcholine: Excitatory (+1, nicotinic receptors).

GABA: Inhibitory (-1, Rdl chloride channels).

Glutamate: Inhibitory (-1 in central nervous system via GluCl-α).

Dopamine/Octopamine: Modulatory (0 in fast ionotropic LIF).

4. Limitations & Non-claims

• Motor neuron firing demonstrates synaptic activation, NOT physical movement. Locomotion requires biomechanical body and muscle models.

• Synapse counts reflect structural contact frequency rather than measured quantal physiological efficacy.
